\section{Life}
People always wonder what is life, why do we live, what is the meaning of our life this chapter 
aims answer those questions

\subsection{Meaning of Life}
Life essentially has no meaning,

\textbf{Biologically} on a higher level you need to pass on your genes on a lower level you live t
maximize your happiness,

\textbf{Socially} you exist to further humanity and make sure that our species survive\\

\textbf{Personally} it's to achieve your own goals, if you don't have any then it's probably to
enjoy life to the fullest. Life doesn't have any meaning, if we choose to live then it's our 
responsibility to find a meaning for your life.

\subsection{Why should we live}
If life doesn't have any meaning then why the fuck should we live right, well you don't have to
but if you choose to end your life why not do whatever you want in life and end it, we all gonna
die someday why not live our life to the fullest. Life is magical it it's own right, In a universe
that exsist to maximize entropy there is life an order out of chaos.

\subsection{How do we live}
Well there is no one certain way to live, I just live to see another day, do what I can to survive
for today, you should also think about long term somtimes but I generally don't bother.

\subsection{How do I live with struggles}
List down all of your problems in a notebook, and strive to do something to solve that problem, you
can ignore the problem if it is impossible to solve as you always ignore what's not in your control

\section{Death}
Death is absense of being, you achieve peace after all your life's interesting paths

\subsection{What happens after we die}
Nothing, you won't have any thought process so you can't process anything, you won't have conciousness, it's like dreamless dream for eternity aka eternal peace. 

\subsection{Why don't we achieve eternal peace early}
Well it's not recommended because, life is an one time opportunity to experience things experience
anything, it's not like we get reincarnated or smth

\section{Acceptance}
One who accepts life and death, accepts peace. To accept life is to accept everyday life's struggle
and to accept death is to accept fear, To accept both is to accept peace.
